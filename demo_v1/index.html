Technology guide

SMF, EFB or AGM — in one look

SMF SEALED MAINTENANCE-FREE

The standard flooded lead-acid battery, sealed so you never top it up. Honest, affordable tech for cars without start-stop.

Cycle life4 / 12

Start-stop: will fail within months

EFB ENHANCED FLOODED

Flooded battery with reinforced plates that survive frequent restarts. The sensible upgrade for basic start-stop cars and heavy city traffic.

Cycle life7 / 12

Start-stop: yes — entry systems

AGM ABSORBENT GLASS MAT

Acid held in glass-mat separators: highest starting power, deep-cycle tolerance, spill-proof. Required by premium start-stop and regenerative braking.

Cycle life12 / 12

Start-stop: yes — required spec

Is your car start-stop? If the engine cuts at red lights and restarts when you release the brake — it is. Fitting a conventional SMF battery to a start-stop car kills it within months and can void the warranty. When in doubt, send us the VIN and we check.

Read your battery

Everything the counter needs is on the label

  • 1
    Capacity — Ah

    Ampere-hours: how much charge the battery stores. Match or exceed the old value.

  • 2
    CCA — cold cranking amps

    Starting power at −18 °C. Higher is fine; lower than the old battery is not.

  • 3
    Date code

    Dots mark month and year of production. We sell stock under 12 months old, always.

  • 4
    Terminal polarity

    Positive left (L+) or right (R+) — the wrong layout will not reach your car’s cables.

Photograph the label and send it on WhatsApp — we decode the rest and quote in one reply.

FAQ

Asked at the counter, answered once

What do CCA and Ah actually mean?

Ah is the tank — how much energy is stored. CCA is the pump — how hard the battery can push on a cold morning. A replacement needs at least the old battery’s numbers in both; more Ah is harmless if the tray fits, more CCA is always safe.

My car has start-stop. Which battery do I need?

EFB for basic start-stop systems, AGM where the car also has regenerative braking or a premium start-stop spec (check your manual or send us the VIN). An ordinary SMF battery will lose capacity within months — it is the most common wrong purchase we see.

How long does a battery last in Tbilisi heat?

Heat, not cold, is what ages a battery — and Tbilisi summers are hard on them. Expect 3–4 years from a quality unit. If yours is past three, a free 5-minute test at the counter tells you what winter will do.

Will you take my old battery?

Yes — with every installation, and on its own too. Buy-back pays 20–40 ₾ by size, and dead batteries are recycled properly rather than ending up in a ravine.
